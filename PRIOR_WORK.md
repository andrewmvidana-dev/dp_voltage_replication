This is the repository's replication target. Its synthetic-voltage/model-fitting pipeline is distinct from this task's direct-load-sum experiment.
- **Verified source:** Borja Balle and Yu-Xiang Wang, [Improving the Gaussian Mechanism for Differential Privacy: Analytical Calibration and Optimal Denoising](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/balle18a.html), ICML 2018. - **Verified source:** Elaine Shi, T-H. - **Verified source:** Christian Mouchet, Elliott Bertrand and Jean-Pierre Hubaux, [An Efficient Threshold Access-Structure for RLWE-Based Multiparty Homomorphic Encryption](https://eprint.iacr.org/2022/780), ePrint 2022/780; journal version in Journal of Cryptology 2023, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00145-023-09452-8). Supplies the threshold-access construction used through Lattigo. Citing the paper does not certify this prototype's concrete parameters or flooding choices.
- **Verified source:** Yucheng Fu and Tianhao Wang, [Benchmarking Secure Sampling Protocols for Differential Privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.10667), 2024. Studies secure noise sampling. Independent local uniform draws in this task are not a maliciously secure joint sampling protocol.
- **Verified source:** Gergely Acs and Claude Castelluccia, [I Have a DREAM! (DiffeRentially privatE smArt Metering)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-24178-9_9), Information Hiding 2011. - **Verified source:** Peter Kairouz, Ziyu Liu and Thomas Steinke, [The Distributed Discrete Gaussian Mechanism for Federated Learning with Secure Aggregation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/kairouz21a.html), ICML 2021. Explains why aggregate protection can be stronger than an individual share's protection, and addresses discrete secure-aggregation arithmetic.
- **Verified source:** Naman Agarwal, Peter Kairouz and Ziyu Liu, [The Skellam Mechanism for Differentially Private Federated Learning](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/285baacbdf8fda1de94b19282acd23e2-Abstract.html), NeurIPS 2021. Uses a distribution closed under summation with secure aggregation. This contrasts with uniforms, whose sum is generally not uniform.
- **Verified source:** [cpSGD: Communication-efficient and differentially-private distributed SGD](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2018/file/21ce689121e39821d07d04faab328370-Paper.pdf), NeurIPS 2018. - **Verified source:** [Compression with Exact Error Distribution for Federated Learning](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/hegazy24a.html), AISTATS 2024. Its discussion of homomorphic quantization includes independent uniform errors whose aggregate is Irwin-Hall. This is a close construction-level comparison, not a claim that its privacy assumptions equal ours.
- **Verified source:** Fabian Laforet, Erik Buchmann and Klemens Bohm, [Towards Provable Privacy Guarantees Using Rechargeable Energy-Storage Devices](https://dbis.ipd.kit.edu/download/eenergy16-battery_privacy.pdf), ACM e-Energy 2016, DOI 10.1145/2934328.2934335. Uses generalized Irwin-Hall perturbations and approximate-DP analysis in an energy setting. Its physical battery and temporal constraints differ from independent message perturbations here.

## Exact scope of the scalar calculation

**ANALYTIC:** For independent Uniform[-b,b] shares, let X be their sum. Symmetry and unimodality give the shift total variation TV(X,X+S)=P(-S/2<X<S/2). With U~IrwinHall(n), X=2bU-nb, hence TV=F_U(n/2+S/(4b))-F_U(n/2-S/(4b)). Numerical inversion of this exact distribution calibrates the ideal scalar release. This does not establish pure DP, a finite-precision implementation theorem, trajectory privacy, or collusion robustness.

**ANALYTIC:** A compromised-key observer that sees all contributions receives the changed meter's single noisy share. Its effective bound is min(1,S/(2b)), rather than the aggregate bound. A central service cannot protect messages that contain the unperturbed meter values. These conclusions assume the observer does not also learn the honest meters' private random draws; learning those draws would allow subtraction.
